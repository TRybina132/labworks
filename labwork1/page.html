Dromaeosaurs

Dromaeosaurs, often called “raptors,” were small to mid-sized feathered predators of the Cretaceous. They walked on two legs and had a large sickle-shaped claw on each foot.

Velociraptor

A turkey-sized raptor with feathered arms and a long, low skull.

Length: about 2 m
Period: Late Cretaceous
Found in: Mongolia, China

Deinonychus

Its discovery in the 1960s changed how scientists thought about dinosaurs, showing them as active and agile.

Length: about 3.4 m
Period: Early Cretaceous
Found in: North America

Utahraptor

One of the largest dromaeosaurs, with a heavy build and a very large sickle claw.

Length: about 6 m
Period: Early Cretaceous
Found in: Utah, USA

Dromaeosaurus

The animal that gave the family its name, with a short, deep skull and a strong bite.

Length: about 1.8 m
Period: Late Cretaceous
Found in: Canada, USA

Microraptor

A crow-sized climber and glider with long feathers on all four limbs.

Length: about 0.8 m
Period: Early Cretaceous
Found in: China

Achillobator

A large raptor with long, strong legs. Its name refers to the Achilles tendon.

Length: about 5 m
Period: Late Cretaceous
Found in: Mongolia

Sizes and dates are approximate.
